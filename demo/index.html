    05

    Limitations

    NDVI indicates vegetation conditions, not organic certification. The blockchain proves that the submitted evidence hash was recorded by an authorized party, not that the underlying environmental claim is necessarily true. This demonstration runs on a local Anvil chain, not a public testnet, and no automatic escrow settlement or payment is demonstrated.
